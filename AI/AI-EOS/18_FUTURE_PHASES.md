# Future Phases

## Current frontend MVP

Static Spanish-first catalog, browser cart, order form, summary, and WhatsApp click-to-chat handoff. No backend or external account integration.

## Future backend phase

- Stored draft orders and order IDs
- Server-side validation
- Integration boundary for a WhatsApp bot to detect website orders

Trigger: the bakery needs reliable order persistence or operational tracking and approves the additional infrastructure.

## Future WhatsApp phase

- WhatsApp-first ordering and interactive menus
- Human escalation and policy-compliant message handling

Trigger: measured message volume and a funded need justify the Business Platform.

## Future payment phase

- Hosted payment links
- Webhook-based status updates
- Automatic confirmations where reliable

Trigger: the client selects and owns a payment provider, and transaction volume justifies integration.

Every future phase requires documented dependencies, risks, migration path, definition of done, and separate approval. A CMS, inventory system, or multi-tenant platform is not implied by this roadmap.
